Celebrate at F&F

Our semi-private dining space accommodates up to 40 guests and is ideal for birthdays, team dinners, holiday parties and milestone celebrations. Groups of 10 or more require a Group Dining Package — served family-style, so everyone at the table gets to share the best of Frankie & Fanucci's.

Menus are customisable within each package, and our team will work with you to make sure your event runs smoothly. Fountain soda is included with every package; bar options are available separately.
