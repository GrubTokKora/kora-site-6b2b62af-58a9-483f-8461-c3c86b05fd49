Menu

Everything at Frankie & Fanucci's in Mamaroneck is made from scratch — Neapolitan thin-crust pies fired in our wood-burning oven, handmade pastas, fresh antipasti, hearty heroes, and desserts worth saving room for. Dine in, take out, or order for delivery.
