Award-winning thin-crust pies, baked in a wood-fired oven. Voted Best of Westchester six times.

Frankie & Fanucci's in Mamaroneck, NY is a vibrant neighbourhood pizzeria known for its award-winning, thin-crust pizzas baked in traditional wood-fired ovens. The menu also features handmade pastas, fresh salads, and Italian favourites. With a full bar, handcrafted cocktails, and a family-friendly vibe, it's perfect for casual nights out or group gatherings.
